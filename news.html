Announcement / Latest

NFP 2 is
official

NFP 2 is Saturday March 27 at the Sheraton Hotel in Nashua, and we want it to be the biggest card we have done yet. The first fight is already signed: Armani Kim vs Jeff Cetoute for the NFP 145 lb Muay Thai Championship, the first Muay Thai title in New Hampshire history.

Fight of the Night pays $2,000, $1,000 to each fighter, and Performance of the Night pays $1,000. Fighter applications are open now and tickets go on sale soon.
