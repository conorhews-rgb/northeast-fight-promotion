What gets you
on the card

We are not looking for a name. We are looking for people who are ready, coachable, and can bring a room with them.

01

You are actually ready

Training consistently under a coach who will vouch for you, and cleared to compete under your state commission.

02

You are easy to find

A record we can verify, footage we can watch, and a coach we can call. Make it easy to say yes.

03

You bring people

Fight nights live or die on the room. Fighters who fill seats get matched first. That is just honest.
