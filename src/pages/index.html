Fights worth
driving to

New England already has the gyms, the coaches and the talent. What it does not have is enough good nights to show it off.

01

Regional talent first

Matchmaking built around New England gyms. If you train here, you fight here, in front of your own room and your own coaches.

02

Production that respects it

Proper lights, proper sound, proper walkouts, proper cameras. An amateur debut should still feel like the biggest night of the year.

03

Fans in the building

Sensible ticket prices, a room that gets loud, and a card you can follow from the first bout to the last. No dead air.

Home base:
the 603

We are setting up in southern New Hampshire. Nashua is the front runner, close enough to Manchester, Lowell and Boston that nobody has an excuse.

  • Venue search under way in the Nashua and greater Manchester area
  • Sanctioned amateur and pro bouts under state athletic commission rules
  • Open to fighters, coaches and officials from across the Northeast
  • Sponsorship and venue partnerships open now
